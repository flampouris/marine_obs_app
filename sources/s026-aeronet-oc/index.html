AERONET-OC

Discover marine observations available through AERONET-OC, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
NASA AERONET
Role
In-situ ocean color calibration/validation
Platforms
Fixed coastal/offshore radiometers
Observed variables
Water-leaving radiance / remote-sensing reflectance; aerosol/atmospheric optical properties
Spatial coverage
Global coastal/island/offshore sites
Period
Site-dependent
Depth coverage
Surface optical signal; no water-column profile
QC and data mode
Level-based QC
Access method
Use AERONET-OC station/product download tools; often accessed as validation source through SeaBASS
