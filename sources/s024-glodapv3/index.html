GLODAPv3

Discover marine observations available through GLODAPv3, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
GLODAP community / NOAA OCADS
Role
Interior ocean BGC synthesis
Platforms
Bottle/CTD hydrography from research cruises
Observed variables
DIC; alkalinity; pH; oxygen; nutrients; salinity; temperature; tracers and carbon-related variables
Spatial coverage
Global open ocean
Period
1972-2023
Depth coverage
Surface to bottom
QC and data mode
Cross-over calibrated and adjusted synthesis
