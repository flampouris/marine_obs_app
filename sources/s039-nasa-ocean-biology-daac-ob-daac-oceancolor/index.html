Discover marine observations available through NASA Ocean Biology DAAC (OB.DAAC) / OceanColor, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
NASA GSFC
Role
Satellite ocean color archive
Platforms
SeaWiFS; MODIS; VIIRS; PACE and related missions
Observed variables
Remote sensing reflectance; chlorophyll; pigments; inherent/apparent optical products; productivity and other ocean-color products
Spatial coverage
Global, cloud-limited
Period
Mission-dependent; SeaWiFS era onward plus current MODIS/VIIRS/PACE
Depth coverage
Surface/upper optical ocean; satellite retrieval, not water-column profile

NASA processing/QC
