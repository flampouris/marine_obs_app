MARINE OBSERVATION SOURCE · S042

NASA NSIDC DAAC

Discover marine observations available through NASA NSIDC DAAC, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
NASA National Snow and Ice Data Center Distributed Active Archive Center
Role
Cryosphere satellite/archive + granule discovery
Platforms
ICESat-2/ATLAS; CryoSat-2/SIRAL; supporting satellite/model products
Observed variables
Sea-ice freeboard, sea-ice thickness, sea-ice elevation, snow depth/density, sea-ice concentration and related cryosphere products
Spatial coverage
Polar/global depending product; marine use primarily Arctic/Antarctic sea ice
Period
Mission/product dependent; ICESat-2 2018-present; CryoSat-2 products 2010-2025
Depth coverage
Sea-ice surface/freeboard/thickness; not a water-column depth profile
QC and data mode
Mission/product-specific validated processing; versioned products
Access method
Discover dataset/granules through NSIDC/Earthdata; filter by space/time; use Earthdata cloud/direct download for files
Indexed download targets
8

Canonical parameters

  • Sea-ice freeboard sea_ice_freeboard
  • Sea-ice thickness sea_ice_thickness

Discovery and access

Add as a first-class cryosphere connector. Index dataset/granule footprints and processing level; do not treat Level-4 thickness analyses as raw direct measurements.
