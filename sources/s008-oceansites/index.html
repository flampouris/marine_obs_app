MARINE OBSERVATION SOURCE · S008

OceanSITES

Discover marine observations available through OceanSITES, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
OceanSITES / GDACs
Role
Reference mooring archive
Platforms
Deep-ocean moorings / fixed observatories
Observed variables
T/S; currents; meteorology; oxygen; carbon/BGC; radiation; sediments and site-specific sensors
Spatial coverage
Sparse global fixed reference sites
Period
Site/deployment dependent; multi-decadal at some sites
Depth coverage
Surface to full mooring instrument depth; sometimes bottom
QC and data mode
Minimal QC for RT/recovered; best available version; OceanSITES QC flags
Access method
Browse OceanSITES GDAC/catalog; download compliant NetCDF
Indexed download targets
