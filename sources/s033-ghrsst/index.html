GHRSST

Discover marine observations available through GHRSST, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
GHRSST international project / NASA PO.DAAC + NCEI
Role
Satellite SST standard + archive
Platforms
Polar-orbiting and geostationary satellite sensors; blended analyses
Observed variables
Sea-surface temperature; uncertainty; quality flags; auxiliary fields
Spatial coverage
Global ocean, sensor/cloud dependent
Period
Product/sensor dependent; multi-decadal archive
Depth coverage
Ocean skin/subskin/foundation SST depending product; surface only
QC and data mode
Standardized GHRSST QC/uncertainty
Access method
Search GHRSST/PO.DAAC collections; use Earthdata granule search/OPeNDAP/S3; prefer L2P for actual observations
