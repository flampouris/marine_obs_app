MARINE OBSERVATION SOURCE · S017

IOOS National Glider DAC

Discover marine observations available through IOOS National Glider DAC, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
U.S. IOOS / NOAA
Role
Glider deployment archive + NRT distribution
Platforms
Underwater gliders
Observed variables
Temperature; salinity/conductivity; pressure; oxygen; chlorophyll; backscatter; currents and mission-specific sensors
Spatial coverage
Primarily U.S.-operated deployments; may include international collaborative missions
Period
Deployment-dependent; 2000s-present
Depth coverage
Surface to mission max depth, often hundreds to ~1000 m
QC and data mode
Provider QC + DAC validation; RT/DM datasets
Access method
Use ERDDAP catalog/search; retrieve dataset metadata from /info/{dataset}; query data with tabledap constraints
Indexed download targets
6
