SOOP / XBT observations via GTSPP and WOD

Discover marine observations available through SOOP / XBT observations via GTSPP and WOD, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

SOOP community / WMO-IOC / national operators
Role
Observation network represented through global archives
Platforms
Ships of opportunity
Observed variables
Primarily temperature profiles (XBT); some XCTD T/S; underway T/S on instrumented ships
Spatial coverage
Global shipping routes / repeat sections
Period
Multi-decadal; source/cruise dependent
Depth coverage
Surface to XBT/XCTD max depth, commonly hundreds to ~1000 m+ depending probe
QC and data mode
Operational + archive QC; XBT bias corrections important

Search WOD dataset=XBT or GTSPP data type; download profiles; enrich platform metadata from OceanOPS SOOP metadata
