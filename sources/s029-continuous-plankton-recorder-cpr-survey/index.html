Continuous Plankton Recorder (CPR) Survey

Discover marine observations available through Continuous Plankton Recorder (CPR) Survey, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
Marine Biological Association and partner surveys
Role
Long-term plankton observation program
Platforms
Ships of opportunity towing CPR samplers
Observed variables
Plankton taxonomy; abundance; ecological indicators
Spatial coverage
Multi-basin; strongest North Atlantic/North Sea, with other regional programs
Period
1931-present, program/region dependent
Depth coverage
Near-surface tow depth; method-specific
QC and data mode
High taxonomic QC
Access method
Use CPR data portal/request process; obtain sample records and metadata
Indexed download targets
2

Canonical parameters

  • Organism quantity organism_quantity
  • Taxon occurrence taxon_occurrence

Discovery and access

Important long historical biological record but access is less API-native than OBIS.
