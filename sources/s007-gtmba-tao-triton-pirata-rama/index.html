GTMBA: TAO/TRITON + PIRATA + RAMA

Discover marine observations available through GTMBA: TAO/TRITON + PIRATA + RAMA, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
NOAA PMEL and international partners
Role
Tropical moored observation network + ERDDAP archive
Platforms
Tropical moored buoys
Observed variables
Ocean temperature; salinity; currents; SST; wind; SLP; air T/RH; shortwave/longwave radiation; heat fluxes; derived thermocline metrics
Spatial coverage
Tropical Pacific, Atlantic, Indian oceans
Period
Combined PMEL archive includes records from late 1970s-present; variable/station dependent
Depth coverage
Surface and multiple subsurface sensor depths
QC and data mode
PMEL/array QC; ERDDAP exposes quality variables
Access method
Use PMEL ERDDAP search/catalog; select dataset then constrain coordinates/time/depth
