MARINE OBSERVATION SOURCE · S019

International Arctic Buoy Programme (IABP)

Discover marine observations available through International Arctic Buoy Programme (IABP), including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
IABP / University of Washington APL
Role
Polar drifting-buoy observations
Platforms
Drifting ice/ocean buoys
Observed variables
Position; barometric pressure; surface temperature; air temperature; hull temperature/battery and other variables in Level 1
Spatial coverage
Arctic
Period
1979-present; hourly after 2016, 3-hour archive 1979-2016
Depth coverage
Surface/ice interface
QC and data mode
Raw daily + Level 1 QC archive with position QC
Access method
API: /download?bid=... with start/end/ndays; or PolarWatch ERDDAP; bulk Level 1 archive
Indexed download targets
3

Canonical parameters

  • Sea-level / barometric pressure air_pressure_at_sea_level
  • Air temperature air_temperature
  • Sea-surface temperature sea_surface_temperature

Discovery and access

Excellent polar metadata and simple per-buoy API.
