MARINE OBSERVATION SOURCE · S027

OBIS

Discover marine observations available through OBIS, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
Ocean Biodiversity Information System / IOC
Role
Marine biodiversity aggregator
Platforms
Surveys; samples; imagery; telemetry; many biological platforms
Observed variables
Species occurrence; abundance/event data; eDNA/DNA extensions; measurements/facts including environmental values where submitted
Spatial coverage
Global
Period
Dataset-dependent; historical to present
Depth coverage
Minimum/maximum depth fields where provided; occurrence/event depth
QC and data mode
OBIS QC pipeline + provider QC
Access method
Small subsets: Mapper; programmatic: API/robis; large-scale app index: download GeoParquet and query locally with DuckDB
Indexed download targets
6

Canonical parameters

  • Biomass biomass
  • Individual count individual_count
  • Occurrence status occurrence_status
  • Organism quantity organism_quantity
  • Observation depth sample_depth
  • Taxon occurrence taxon_occurrence

Discovery and access
