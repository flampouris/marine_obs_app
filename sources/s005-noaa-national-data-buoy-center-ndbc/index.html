MARINE OBSERVATION SOURCE · S005

NOAA National Data Buoy Center (NDBC)

Discover marine observations available through NOAA National Data Buoy Center (NDBC), including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
NOAA NDBC
Role
Observation network + archive
Platforms
Moored buoys; drifting buoys; C-MAN/coastal stations; partner platforms
Observed variables
Wind; pressure; air temperature; humidity/dew point; SST; wave height/period/direction; spectra; currents; oceanographic variables; tide; solar radiation
Spatial coverage
Primarily U.S. waters plus partner stations distributed more broadly
Period
Station-dependent; many records from 1970s-present
Depth coverage
Surface; sensor-specific subsurface; ADCP profiles where present
QC and data mode
Automated QC for real time; post-processed historical archive
Access method
Realtime files: /data/realtime2/{station}.{type}; historical directories by data type/year; DODS for NetCDF
Indexed download targets
