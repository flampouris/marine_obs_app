MARINE OBSERVATION SOURCE · S036

Copernicus Marine satellite observations

Discover marine observations available through Copernicus Marine satellite observations, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
Copernicus Marine Service
Role
Harmonized satellite observation products
Platforms
Multiple satellite missions
Observed variables
SST; sea level/altimetry; waves; ocean color; sea ice; winds and other ocean ECVs depending catalogue
Spatial coverage
Global + regional seas
Period
Product-specific; NRT + multi-year
Depth coverage
Mostly surface; some sea-ice/altimetry-derived vertical-like quantities
QC and data mode
Producer + Copernicus QC
Access method
Use catalogue `describe`; use `subset` by variable/bbox/time; use `get` for original files
