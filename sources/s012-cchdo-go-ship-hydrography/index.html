MARINE OBSERVATION SOURCE · S012

CCHDO / GO-SHIP hydrography

Discover marine observations available through CCHDO / GO-SHIP hydrography, including parameters, platforms, spatial and temporal coverage, metadata, and access methods.

Operator
CCHDO / GO-SHIP community
Role
Reference ship hydrography archive
Platforms
Research vessels / CTD-rosette casts
Observed variables
CTD T/S/O2; bottle DIC; alkalinity; pH; nutrients; tracers; many BGC variables
Spatial coverage
Global repeat hydrographic sections
Period
Cruise-dependent; broadly 1970s-present, with WOCE/CLIVAR/GO-SHIP eras
Depth coverage
Surface to seafloor / full ocean depth
QC and data mode
Reference-quality hydrography with strong curation
Access method
Use Advanced Search/Map Search for cruises or ERDDAP for profile-level filters and download
Indexed download targets
15
